Eligibility Criteria

Check if you qualify for MBBS admission in European medical universities. Requirements vary slightly by country — our counsellors can give you personalised guidance.

🎓

Academic Qualification

  • Completed 10+2 / A-Levels / High School
  • Physics, Chemistry & Biology as main subjects
  • Minimum 50% aggregate (60% for Indian General category)
  • Final results or predicted grades accepted
📝

Entrance Exam

  • NEET qualification required for Indian students
  • Pakistan: MDCAT or NUST entry test
  • Some universities require Biology/Chemistry entrance
  • No entrance exam for many EU universities
🗣️

Language Proficiency

  • IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 72+ for English programs
  • German B2/C1 required for most German programs
  • Many universities accept high school English proof
  • Mother-tongue English speakers may be exempt
🎂

Age Requirements

  • Minimum age: 17 years at time of admission
  • Maximum age: 25 years (varies by country)
  • No upper age limit in some European countries
  • Age as per passport will be considered

Required Documents

Prepare these documents well in advance. Requirements may vary slightly by university — our team will provide a personalised checklist.

🎓

Academic Documents

Class 10 mark sheet & certificate, Class 12 mark sheet & certificate, NEET scorecard (for Indian students), MDCAT result (Pakistan students).

🌍

Identity Documents

Valid passport (minimum 3 years validity), passport-size photographs (white background), national ID card or birth certificate.

📞

English Proficiency

IELTS, TOEFL or equivalent certificate OR school medium of instruction certificate in English from your school/college principal.

🏥

Medical Documents

Medical fitness certificate from a registered doctor, HIV test report (required by some countries), vaccination records.

💰

Financial Documents

Bank statement showing sufficient funds (typically 12 months of tuition + living costs), financial sponsorship letter if applicable.

📄

Supporting Documents

Statement of Purpose (SOP), motivation letter, character certificate from school, recommendation letters (some universities).
