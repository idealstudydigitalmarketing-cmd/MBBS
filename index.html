FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about studying MBBS in Europe.

Yes. MBBS degrees from WHO and NMC-listed European universities are valid in India, Pakistan, Nepal, and other South Asian countries. However, you must clear the FMGE (India), PMDC screening test (Pakistan), or respective licensing exams to practice in your home country.

Indian students must qualify NEET to study MBBS abroad as per NMC regulations (from 2021). However, the specific NEET score requirement for European countries is lower than for Indian medical colleges. Contact us for the current year's requirements.

The duration of MBBS (MD Program) in most European countries is 6 years. This includes 5 years of theoretical and clinical study followed by 1 year of practical clinical internship at the affiliated university hospital.

Most European countries (Poland, Hungary, Czech Republic, Romania, Latvia) offer English-medium MBBS programs specifically designed for international students. Germany has some English programs but many require German language proficiency.

Yes! Several universities offer merit-based scholarships and fee waivers for international students. EU governments also offer Erasmus+ scholarships. Contact our counsellors to learn about current scholarship opportunities.

Yes, Europe is very safe for international students. Most European countries have large communities of Indian, Pakistani, Nepali, and African students. Universities have dedicated international student offices and cities like Kraków, Budapest, and Prague are extremely student-friendly.
